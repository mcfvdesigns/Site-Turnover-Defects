# Governance & Compliance Checklist

## 1. Privacy & Consent
* **Anonymization & Worker Privacy:** All inspection images uploaded to the platform must be screened to ensure site personnel, faces, personal items, or identifying property markers (e.g., unit numbers, address plates) are either excluded or blurred prior to storage and training.

## 2. Data Minimization
* **Targeted Spatial Scope:** Data collection is strictly restricted to specific surface defect regions (e.g., wall joins, door jambs, tile surfaces, ceiling sections). Wide-angle ambient room photography containing non-essential property assets is avoided.

## 3. Limitations Statement
* **Non-Structural Advisory Tool:** This model is designed strictly as an automated screening assistant for cosmetic and superficial turnover punchlists. 
* **Prohibited Use Cases:** It must **not** be relied upon for primary structural integrity assessments, load-bearing compliance certifications, or legally binding handover sign-offs without secondary verification by a licensed structural engineer or QA inspector.

## 4. Risk Assessment & Operational Costs
* **False Positives (High Precision Risk):** Misclassifying benign shadows or dirt as defects incurs a low-to-moderate operational cost (unnecessary manual re-inspections by site managers).
* **False Negatives (High Recall Risk):** Overlooking real handover defects (e.g., active moisture leaks, cracked tiles) poses a significantly higher quality and financial risk, as unresolved defects lead to post-turnover client disputes and costly warranty claims.
